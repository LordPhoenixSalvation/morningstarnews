Working the birds

FM birds (SO-50, AO-91, AO-92, PO-101): 2m up, 70cm down. SO-50 needs a 74.4 Hz CTCSS tone to open. Full-duplex or two radios beats one — you need to hear yourself coming back. Doppler shifts about ±3 kHz on 70cm through a pass, so tune as it moves.
Linear birds (RS-44): invert the transponder — LSB on the uplink becomes USB on the downlink. Best from 5° elevation upward.
ISS: voice on 145.800 FM during school contacts, APRS on 145.825. Listen before you transmit.
