Today’s Edition

Google shipped its November update a month early and broke the lock screen

Compiled by the Rool Machine · Sources linked

Pixel QPR2 arrives ahead of schedule with 33 fixes and a few new problems, Children in Need tops £38m, and the Orks get a codex.

UK news

Children in Need raises £38.7m - Friday night’s broadcast closed on £38,720,170, with the total still climbing as donation lines stayed open. A grim week for the country and a good one for the charity: the money goes out to projects across the UK within the year.

Winter fuel payments start landing - Pensioners in England, Wales and Northern Ireland begin receiving £100 to £300 this month, with anyone earning over £35,000 having it clawed back through their tax code. The compromise nobody loves, administered quietly. source

UK television

I’m A Celebrity returns tomorrow - The 2026 line-up arrives on ITV1 and STV on 16 November, which means three weeks of the national conversation being about eating things in a forest. source

Adultery, ITV - Danny Brocklehurst’s new drama, led by Dominic Cooper and Romola Garai, heads ITV’s November slate. Brocklehurst wrote Ordinary Lies and Safe, so expect the affair to be the least of anyone’s problems. source

Pokémon

A countdown to 30 November - The Pokémon Company’s Japanese site is running an unexplained countdown that expires at 2pm GMT on the 30th, under the 30th-anniversary logos. The leading theory is a mainline announcement rather than merchandise, which would be the first real news of the anniversary year. source

The November TCG calendar - The Pokémon Company has published the full list of TCG product releases for the month, which is the useful document if you’re trying to work out what’s actually out versus what’s a retailer placeholder. source

Tabletop games

The 11th edition Ork codex has leaked - Rumours point to new Nobz, a rebuilt Battlewagon and an imminent release window, with the codex itself circulating ahead of any official word. Treat leaked rules as entertainment until Games Workshop says otherwise, but the model rumours have been consistent for weeks. source

D&D Beyond’s November drop goes to Ysgard - This month’s content drop is themed on the Upper Plane of Ysgard and adds three creatures including the twin-headed Dread Linnorm at CR 17, plus maps and stickers for DMs. Free with a subscription, and the monsters are the reason to look. source

Sci-fi and fantasy

“We Are The Borg” is a Commander deck - One of four Commander decks in the Magic: The Gathering x Star Trek set, announced today as part of the franchise’s 60th anniversary. Sixty years of Trek, and the Borg still get top billing over the actual crews. source

Star Trek Online’s anniversary crossover - The game is marking the 60th with a crossover event pulling characters across the franchise’s eras. The only place this year where the timelines are allowed to collide on purpose. source

Tech

AI - Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 take the index lead - Anthropic’s pair shipped on 9 November as one model under two safeguard regimes, debuting top of the new Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index at 65, five points clear. The interesting part is the split: same weights, different rules for what it will discuss. source

AI - Gemini 4 Hydrogen - Google’s efficient multimodal reasoning model landed on 12 November and has been rolling out since. The pitch is capability per unit of compute rather than a benchmark crown. source

Linux - 7.3 is the 2026 LTS, and OpenSSH needs patching - Greg Kroah-Hartman confirmed 7.3 will be the long-term support kernel rather than 7.4, settling the baseline for next year’s hardware support packages. Separately, OpenSSH 10.6p2 closes a critical password-authentication bypass, so patch that today. source

Linux - a mandatory upgrade advisory - Kernel 6.12.117 came out on 10 November carrying the “all users must upgrade” wording, which the stable team reserves for memory-safety and security fixes serious enough to say out loud. source

Android - the November Pixel Drop, a month early - Android 17 QPR2 rolled out with the November Feature Drop and security patch, bringing Gemini Intelligence to the Pixel 10, a widget builder, an AI maths solver and 33 bug fixes. Shipping a quarterly release early is unusual, and the reason matters more than the features. source

Android - and it’s breaking things - Users are reporting dead always-on displays, lock screen problems and missing notifications after the update. The standard advice applies: if your phone is working and you don’t need the new widget builder, wait for the point release. source

macOS - 27.2.1 Golden Gate, plus two older branches - Apple shipped macOS 27.2.1 with bug and security fixes on 9 November, two weeks after 27.2, alongside Tahoe 26.7.3 and Sequoia 15.8.3. Three branches patched at once is Apple doing maintenance properly for once. source

macOS - HomeView and a new Apple TV 4K - Both released on 13 November after a fortnight of pre-orders. The HomeView is the more interesting object: a screen for the house rather than a device for a person. source

The Pixel update is the week in miniature: shipped ahead of schedule, packed with capability, and quietly breaking the one thing people rely on without thinking about it. Nobody notices the lock screen until it stops working.

Automation is a promise that someone else has thought about the edge cases. Usually they haven’t, and usually you find out at 3am when the thing that was meant to be simple has become the whole job.
