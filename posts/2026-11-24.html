UK NEWS

The disability benefit review says Pip should stay cash, but stop feeling like an interrogation - Sir Stephen Timms publishes his review today, and says he will recommend that personal independence payment remains a “cash-first benefit” while letting some claimants choose services instead. The wider ask is to “rehumanise” a process that the minister’s own colleagues have spent two years apologising for. source

Burnham tells the Commons his EU approach is pragmatic, and that a referendum would be “unthinkable” - The prime minister used Monday’s statement to insist the new agreements on food exports, emissions trading and a youth scheme are “not a prelude to anything”, with “no fixed destination” and no banging on about Europe. Kemi Badenoch said he had oversold the deal and was trying to take Britain back to 2015; six former prime ministers have now had a go at him in a week, which is either a record or a hobby. source

UK TELEVISION

The BBC’s Christmas schedule arrives, and Doctor Foster is coming back - Suranne Jones returns to the role after ten years, with a Call the Midwife prequel called Sisters in Arms, Kit Harington in A Tale of Two Cities, Miranda Hart hosting Celebrity Parlour Games, a Ghost Story for Christmas with Jim Broadbent and Martin Freeman, and an Aardman fiftieth-birthday retrospective. There is also a new Adrian Mole. The corporation has clearly decided the country has had enough reality for one year. source

POKEMON

Thirty years of Pokémon gets a music video, and the countdown ends - A month of cryptic Japanese social posts resolved into Evergreen, a commemorative song by Official Hige Dandism with animation by Madhouse, pushed across every Pokémon account at once. No game announcement, no tenth-generation trailer: just a pop band and a very expensive cartoon, which after a year of anniversary releases is almost restful. source

TABLETOP GAMES

Dawn of War IV gets its last trailer before the shooting starts - Relic’s return to the 40k real-time strategy series opens in early access through the Commander Edition on 30 November, with general release on 3 December. To mark it, Warhammer stores are handing out a game desk mat this weekend to anyone spending £95, which is roughly one and a half boxes of Orks and therefore no hardship at all. source

SCI-FI AND FANTASY

Star Trek’s Magic: The Gathering set goes competitive on Arena - The Universes Beyond cards entered MTG Arena a week ago, and yesterday brought the Contender Draft: 3,000 gems or 20,000 gold, seven wins or three losses, and prize support that pays nothing at all below three wins. A high-risk structure for a set where the Borg Queen is a legal commander and Worf has his own deck, which tells you who the design team were afraid of. source

TECH

AI: Nvidia’s board reinstates Jensen Huang, citing government input - Huang is back as president, chief executive and a director with immediate effect, after a board committee spent weeks looking into reporting that he had been the target of a foreign intelligence effort. The board says it received input from senior levels of the US government and is satisfied his return poses no national security risk; Colette Kress, interim chief since the affair broke, goes back to being chief financial officer full time, and the investigation into the original reporting continues regardless. source

Linux: CachyOS ships a separate kernel just for anti-cheat games - The November ISO adds Easy Anti-Cheat Linux v2 support through a dedicated signed kernel with its own boot entry, plus a recovery environment that can roll back Btrfs snapshots without a live USB. The catch is the list of conditions: Secure Boot with a key Epic trusts, TPM 2.0, IOMMU enabled and kernel lockdown. Being allowed to play the game is now a compliance exercise. source

Hardware: Meshtastic’s Android app gets a guided setup and an AR signal survey - The v2.8.5-closed.1 build adds a walkthrough for commissioning a new node, a signal survey with live map overlay, heatmap and augmented-reality view, a telemetry widget, bootloader upgrades for nRF52 boards over Bluetooth, and channel sharing that can replace or add. Also a “banana for scale”. Radio people will understand. source

The day’s theme is people being put back where they were: Huang in his chair, Foster in her consulting room, Pip in a form that at least pretends to be about a person. Only the Borg Queen is new, and she is charging 3,000 gems.
