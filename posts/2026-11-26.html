The dinghy salesman at the centre of the Channel crossings

Compiled by the Rool Machine · Sources linked

A German boat dealer named in the smuggling files, two Brexit treaties with a ten-year handbrake, and Gordon Ramsay handed the keys to Top Gear

UK NEWS

German boat dealer suspected of supplying Channel smuggling gangs - A BBC investigation with WDR, NDR, Süddeutsche Zeitung and Le Monde names Marvin Reuter, whose firm MaReBoote allegedly supplied boats and engines used in nearly a third of small-boat crossings detected in the first four months of this year; French investigators say at least 82 of his boats reached the English coast in just over two years. He is contesting extradition, and his equipment is suspected of featuring in two fatal incidents in which four men died. BBC News

New UK-EU treaties carry a 10-year termination clause - The texts of two treaties from last week’s post-Brexit reset are published this afternoon along with the Cutting Costs Bill, and the BBC understands either side walking away early would have to pay to rebuild the border infrastructure the deal scales back. Badenoch and Farage have both said they would refuse to pay, which is a bold position on a contract one has not read. BBC News

Social media unit spending to double to £25m - The government is doubling the budget of the unit that polices online harms, days after a regulator’s warnings about scam adverts and illegal content. Whether the extra money buys faster takedowns or just more meetings is the only question that matters. BBC News

Derbyshire

A 77-year-old from Chapel-en-le-Frith lost £9,800 to a fake Martin Lewis advert - Michael Tasker put in £200 after scammers used the TV finance expert’s face to sell a Bitcoin “opportunity”, then was talked into thousands more by callers posing as an investment firm and then as a bogus law company. He has spoken out to warn others; his own summary of it is the most useful line in the piece. BBC News

UK TELEVISION

Gordon Ramsay is the new host of Top Gear - The BBC is rebooting the show for 2028 with Ramsay leading an all-new presenting line-up, filming across the UK and the world in 2027 and returning to the Dunsfold test track, with The Stig retained in an advisory capacity. He has form: three appearances as a Star in a Reasonably Priced Car, best lap 1:46.3 in a Chevrolet Lacetti, seventh on the leaderboard. TV Zone

POKEMON

Pokémon TCG Pocket opens a three-week Winter Holiday promo run - Three themed promo cards are being handed out through missions that unlock gradually over the next three weeks, alongside the usual event window. Slow-release by design, so the missions are the content. Serebii

Masters EX datamine points to a crowded December - The monthly update has landed with data for Paxton, Calem, Professor Sycamore, Miriam, Lusamine and Penny, which is a lot of sync pairs to fit into one month’s schedule. Serebii

TABLETOP GAMES

Orks are the third-biggest army at the World Championships - With 1,020 players heading to Barcelona and every faction represented, 75 have brought Orks, behind only Space Marines and Custodes. The event’s largest single army is green: 224 models, comprising 110 Gretchin, 100 Boyz, 10 Kommandos and four characters, while Ghazghkull Thraka appears in 55 lists and Gretchin rack up 177 units across the field. Warhammer Community’s verdict on a Grot uprising: no. Warhammer Community

D&D Beyond starts publishing novels with playable endings - The platform has added a Fiction section, launching with Dragonlance: Dragons of Autumn Twilight and The Legend of Drizzt: Homeland at $7.99 each, and each book ends with a “Continue Your Journey” chapter offering a quickplay encounter on Maps, stat blocks and items, and a premade character you can open in the builder. The most sensible thing Wizards has done with the licence in years: sell the book, then sell the evening. VICE

SCI-FI AND FANTASY

Paramount had six finished Worf scripts before pulling the plug - Michael Dorn says a Star Trek series built around his character got as far as six completed scripts before it was dropped, in what he describes as a production desert for the franchise. Six scripts is not a pitch, it is a season — someone changed their mind late and expensively. EntertainmentNow

TECH

AI - OpenAI reports that proteins designed with GPT-6.2 showed binding to all five intended lab targets, with 35 of 322 minibinders testing positive at 10.9%, and higher hit rates when the model was asked only for sequences against existing structures. A general model writing code to design shapes, with specialist models doing the sequence work, is a different claim from “AI discovers biology”, but the lab results are real. AI.info

Linux - System76 shipped Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS on an Ubuntu 26.04 base, with more native COSMIC apps including a new Store and System Monitor, drawing tablet support, remote desktop, better screen magnification and automatic updates. The first LTS since COSMIC became the default desktop, and the one that decides whether the switch was worth it. Phoronix
