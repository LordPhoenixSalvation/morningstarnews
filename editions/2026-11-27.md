A party split over oil, migration at its lowest since 1997, and a Midlands road scheme quietly binned to pay for defence.

## UK NEWS

**Ninety-two backbenchers and four frontbenchers sign the Rosebank letter** - Labour's split over the biggest undeveloped oilfield in UK waters is now on paper, with MPs telling Andy Burnham that approving it would "permanently damage" the party's climate credibility and its election chances. The Treasury wants the investment; a large chunk of the parliamentary party wants it refused. Only one of those can win. [source](https://www.theguardian.com/politics/2026/nov/26/labour-split-over-rosebank-oilfield-exposed-by-letter-from-nearly-100-mps)

**Net migration falls to its lowest level since 1997, pandemics aside** - The drop is sharp enough that even the government's critics are arguing about the cause rather than the number, with ministers crediting policy and analysts pointing at a cooling labour market. Either way it hands Burnham a rare piece of good news on an issue he has been losing sleep over. [source](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2026/nov/26/net-migration-to-uk-falls-to-lowest-level-since-1997-excluding-pandemic)

**Up to 1.4 million workers may miss out on the zero-hours shake-up** - Unions say the protections are being drafted in a way that leaves the people on the most precarious contracts outside them, which would be a remarkable own goal for a reform designed to fix exactly that. [source](https://www.theguardian.com/uk-news/2026/nov/27/up-to-1point4-million-uk-workers-risk-missing-out-zero-hours-shake-up)

### Derbyshire

**The £600m A38 relief road in Derby is cancelled** - The government has shelved the long-planned scheme to ease congestion around the city, with the money redirected towards defence. Derby has been waiting on this road for years and will now be waiting rather longer. [source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cq5y1p51x5pmo)
