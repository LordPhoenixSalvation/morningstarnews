## UK NEWS

**German boat dealer suspected of supplying Channel smuggling gangs** - A BBC investigation with WDR, NDR, Süddeutsche Zeitung and Le Monde names Marvin Reuter, whose firm MaReBoote allegedly supplied boats and engines used in nearly a third of small-boat crossings detected in the first four months of this year; French investigators say at least 82 of his boats reached the English coast in just over two years. He is contesting extradition, and his equipment is suspected of featuring in two fatal incidents in which four men died. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cqlymky8kpzjo)

**New UK-EU treaties carry a 10-year termination clause** - The texts of two treaties from last week's post-Brexit reset are published this afternoon along with the Cutting Costs Bill, and the BBC understands either side walking away early would have to pay to rebuild the border infrastructure the deal scales back. Badenoch and Farage have both said they would refuse to pay, which is a bold position on a contract one has not read. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c98r7p65edvgo)

**Social media unit spending to double to £25m** - The government is doubling the budget of the unit that polices online harms, days after a regulator's warnings about scam adverts and illegal content. Whether the extra money buys faster takedowns or just more meetings is the only question that matters. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c62l5j02lj0go)
