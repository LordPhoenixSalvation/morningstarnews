## AI

**Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO on 12 November, effective immediately**, after the board opened an investigation into an affair with a woman reported to be an alleged Chinese intelligence asset. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO. He's been summoned to testify before a Senate committee next month. [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/12/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns) · [AP](https://apnews.com/article/nvidia-megaspeed-china-singapore-huang-5403f152a601cf82ce939f1d24fffa0f)

**Anthropic shipped Claude Fable 5.2 on 9 November** — built for multi-day autonomous coding with a 1M-token window and a 20% price cut. Google answered on the 12th with Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a cheap high-throughput workhorse at $1.50 per million input tokens. [AI Spark Hub weekly recap](https://aisparkhub.com/weekly-recap-nov-8-nov-14-2026/)

**The Guardian found an "AI clog" paralysing UK councils** — citizens generating 40-page legal appeals in seconds, staff required by law to read every one. [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/08/ai-clog-public-bodies-local-authorities-onslaught-chatbot-generated-correspondence)

*Why it matters:* the AI boom lost the one person who was its public face, in the same week the technology started visibly breaking public administration.

## Linux

**Linux 7.4-rc2 shipped on 9 November** after what Torvalds calls the largest merge window in kernel history — nearly 20,000 commits against the usual 15,000. [Linux Compatible](https://www.linuxcompatible.org/story/linux-kernel-74rc2-released-largest-merge-window-in-history-meets-stabilization-test/)

**Torvalds says the project has found a "much healthier model" for AI contributions** — machine-made patches arriving during merge windows rather than clogging the stabilisation period, though "they often get rewritten". A third of the changes this cycle are AMD GPU register offsets. [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/os-platforms/2026/11/01/linus-torvalds-says-linux-kernel-is-out-of-stabilization-phase-and-using-much-healthier-model-for-ai-contributions/5306852)

**Another kernel developer has stepped back from duties citing overload**, and LWN's weekly edition leads on the "kernel CVE flood". Security releases went out on the 10th for 7.3.4, 6.18.63 and 6.12.117, the last carrying a mandatory-upgrade advisory. [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/11/13/another-linux-kernel-developer-steps-back-from-some-duties-due-to-overload/5309628) · [LWN](https://lwn.net/) · [Linux Compatible](https://www.linuxcompatible.org/story/linux-kernel-612117-released-with-mandatory-upgrade-advisory/)

*Why it matters:* the kernel is simultaneously absorbing more AI-generated code and losing the humans who review it. Torvalds sounds optimistic. The resignation notices sound less so.
