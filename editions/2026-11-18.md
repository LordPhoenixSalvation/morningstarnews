A 12-month high in the cost of living, a river you cannot swim in, and a great many websites quietly serving malware through a tool they all trusted.

## UK NEWS

**Inflation climbs to 3.6%, a 12-month high, and the pumps are to blame** — The Consumer Prices Index rose 3.6% in the year to October, up from 3.5% in September, the ONS said this morning. Petrol went up 5.2p a litre between September and October to 174.7p, the highest since August 2022. Food and services were the drag the other way; fuel did the damage. The Bank of England meets next month with the rate still above target and the direction of travel now sideways rather than down. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmy498kdkx3xo)

**Britain's worst bathing site, and the sewage works next door** — The BBC sampled the water upstream of French Weir Park in Taunton, named the worst in the country with a 68% failure rate for faecal bacteria, and found the treated liquid coming out of the nearest works at Bradford-on-Tone carrying around 240,000 colony forming units of E. coli per 100ml. Treated, in this context, is doing a great deal of work. Wessex Water says it may be agricultural. Both things are probably true. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cm752w35lkz3o)

### Derbyshire

**Nearly 200 sites, D&D Beyond among them, served card-skimming malware through Optimizely** — An attacker injected code into the "Project JavaScript" panel of customer projects on the website-testing platform, and because it came from Optimizely's own trusted servers every affected site loaded it. Report URI counted 178 organisations: Zoom, the BBC, CNN, Salesforce, IKEA, HSBC, and Wizards of the Coast's Magic storefront Secret Lair. The script was updated 26 times across the day, ending up swapping crypto wallet addresses, skimming card fields and pushing exploits at older iPhones. Optimizely patched it and the clean-up finished by the evening of the 16th. Wizards has said nothing publicly. If you bought anything on D&D Beyond on Monday, check your statement. [D&D Fanatics](https://dungeonsanddragonsfan.com/dnd-beyond-malware-attack/)

## TECH

**AI** — OpenAI shipped GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, and its own system card is the story: both sit at the Critical cybersecurity threshold, both at High for biological and chemical risk, and Astra also reaches High for AI self-improvement. Microsoft came out the same day with MAI Code 2 and licensed model weights for Azure customers. [AI Impact Hub](https://www.aiimpacthub.com/ai-news/2026-11-18)

Everything this morning is a bill arriving from a system someone else was supposed to be maintaining.
