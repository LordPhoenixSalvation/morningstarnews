# Ground Rent, Meet the Peppercorn

Leasehold is to be abolished in all but name, the Bayesian inquiry finds a risk nobody had assessed, and Anthropic goes public at a number that stops conversation.

## UK NEWS

**'Feudal' leasehold system set to be scrapped in England and Wales** — The Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Bill is published today and introduced to Parliament, banning most new leasehold flats in favour of commonhold, where residents collectively own the building. Ground rent for existing leaseholders is capped at £250 from 2028 and falls to a peppercorn after 40 years. Housing minister Matthew Pennycook called the current system "anachronism in the 21st century"; freeholders, including housing associations and the pension funds holding the ground rents, call it an "unprecedented abuse" of their rights that will wipe billions off portfolios. It will not be law before next year at the earliest, and both Houses get a say first. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw987ynpd0n1o)

**Crew unaware of risks before Mike Lynch's yacht Bayesian sank, say investigators** — The Marine Accident Investigation Branch's final report finds the superyacht had a vulnerability to knockdown that had never been assessed with its sails down at anchor, so nobody aboard knew how easily she could go over in the squall that killed seven people off Sicily in August 2024, including Mike Lynch and his daughter Hannah. The centreboard is at the centre of it. The findings sit awkwardly beside the Italian criminal inquiry, which has taken a different view of who was responsible. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c6e9kn15z9d7o)

**Anglo-Saxon 'sand body' among new Sutton Hoo finds** — Time Team's dig in the Garden Field cemetery has uncovered a grave where the body survives not as bone but as an impression pressed into the sand, along with beads, tweezers, a knife and a brooch, and evidence of a second burial beneath it. The excavation is the first serious work beyond the royal mounds in decades, and it suggests the cemetery was far busier than the famous ship burial implied. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c39w95dnyrlvo)

## TABLETOP GAMES

**Space Marines have lost their last aircraft, and every other flyer is now on notice** — The new codex sends the Stormhawk Interceptor, Stormtalon Gunship and Stormraven Gunship to Legends in one stroke, and Games Workshop has said this is how removals will work from now on. Every codex on the 11th edition schedule is therefore another chance for somebody's plane to go the same way, and Ork players should read the Heldrake precedent closely: the dataslate stripped its Aircraft keyword, set its OC to zero and gave it a normal 12" move rather than retiring the model. If a Dakkajet can be quietly turned into a very fast, very ugly ground vehicle, it will be. [Spikey Bits](https://spikeybits.com/40k-flyers-going-to-legends-next-in-11th-edition/)

**GW has capped allocations on the Christmas Battleforces everyone actually wants** — Retailers say the Chaos Knights Helhunt Lance, Chaos Space Marines Cult of Destruction and the two Horus Heresy battle groups are being limited per store, while sales reps ring round offering extra copies of the rest after the 4 November order deadline. The Orks Armoured Blitzmob — Mega Armoured Warboss, Battlewagon, Deff Dread, Meganobz and Boyz — is not on the short list, so it should be findable. The army that just anchored a new edition being the easy one to buy is its own small indignity. [Spikey Bits](https://spikeybits.com/christmas-boxes-allocation-hits-the-battleforces-everyone-wants/)

## TECH

**AI** — Anthropic priced its IPO at $83 a share and raised $81.3bn, the largest stock market debut on record, valuing the five-year-old company at $1.5tn ahead of trading on the Nasdaq today. The offering was upsized, which is the detail that matters: demand was not the problem. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6742m39xgzxo)

**Linux** — 7.4-rc3 is out, a quiet round dominated by networking and GPU driver fixes plus a large iomap calling-convention change, with the final release still pointing at late December. The merge window behind it was the largest on record at 19,671 commits, so a calm rc3 is better news than it sounds. [Phoronix](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Linux-7.4-rc3-Released)

**Android** — The November Google System Updates are landing, and the interesting one is the Pixel virtual gamepad, which finally gives foldables a sensible way to play. Play Store, Play services and Wallet changes ride alongside, plus a new family space in Settings. [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/16/november-2026-google-system-updates/)

**Hardware** — ESP-IDF v6.1.1 shipped on Monday, a maintenance release on the 6.1 line for anyone building against Espressif's toolchain. Nothing dramatic; update if you are on 6.1, and note the Meshtastic firmware beta has not moved since June. [ESP-IDF releases](https://github.com/espressif/esp-idf/releases/tag/v6.1.1)

So: the law arrives to end a system it will take forty years to unwind, the report arrives to explain a sinking nobody could have avoided because nobody had been told, and a five-year-old company is now worth more than the entire stock market of most countries. Everything today is paperwork catching up with something that already happened.
