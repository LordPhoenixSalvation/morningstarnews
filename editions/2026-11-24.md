## UK NEWS

**The disability benefit review says Pip should stay cash, but stop feeling like an interrogation** - Sir Stephen Timms publishes his review today, and says he will recommend that personal independence payment remains a "cash-first benefit" while letting some claimants choose services instead. The wider ask is to "rehumanise" a process that the minister's own colleagues have spent two years apologising for. [source](https://www.impartialreporter.com/news/national/26755993.review-will-recommend-changes-rehumanise-disability-benefit-says-minister/)

**Burnham tells the Commons his EU approach is pragmatic, and that a referendum would be "unthinkable"** - The prime minister used Monday's statement to insist the new agreements on food exports, emissions trading and a youth scheme are "not a prelude to anything", with "no fixed destination" and no banging on about Europe. Kemi Badenoch said he had oversold the deal and was trying to take Britain back to 2015; six former prime ministers have now had a go at him in a week, which is either a record or a hobby. [source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c6vg2v9xkr40o)

## TECH

**AI: Nvidia's board reinstates Jensen Huang, citing government input** - Huang is back as president, chief executive and a director with immediate effect, after a board committee spent weeks looking into reporting that he had been the target of a foreign intelligence effort. The board says it received input from senior levels of the US government and is satisfied his return poses no national security risk; Colette Kress, interim chief since the affair broke, goes back to being chief financial officer full time, and the investigation into the original reporting continues regardless. [source](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director)

**Linux: CachyOS ships a separate kernel just for anti-cheat games** - The November ISO adds Easy Anti-Cheat Linux v2 support through a dedicated signed kernel with its own boot entry, plus a recovery environment that can roll back Btrfs snapshots without a live USB. The catch is the list of conditions: Secure Boot with a key Epic trusts, TPM 2.0, IOMMU enabled and kernel lockdown. Being allowed to play the game is now a compliance exercise. [source](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2026/11/cachyos-november-2026-release-brings-support-for-easy-anti-cheat-linux-v2/)

**Hardware: Meshtastic's Android app gets a guided setup and an AR signal survey** - The v2.8.5-closed.1 build adds a walkthrough for commissioning a new node, a signal survey with live map overlay, heatmap and augmented-reality view, a telemetry widget, bootloader upgrades for nRF52 boards over Bluetooth, and channel sharing that can replace or add. Also a "banana for scale". Radio people will understand. [source](https://github.com/meshtastic/Meshtastic-Android/releases)
