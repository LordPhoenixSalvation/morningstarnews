## UK NEWS

**The DWP's systems are still down, and nobody will say why** - Almost every Department for Work and Pensions online service remained offline on Saturday morning, a day after ministers took them down over what Work and Pensions Secretary Pat McFadden called a "serious cyber incident". Payments are reportedly unaffected; the universal credit portal very much is. Downing Street has declined to rule out a malicious actor, which is the sort of sentence that does the opposite of reassurance. [source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c8wy5p6pv2e2o)

**Six bidders, one Poundland, until Sunday** - Administrators have set a weekend deadline for offers for the collapsed discount chain, with all 600-odd stores still trading and roughly 11,000 jobs hanging on who blinks first. Staff told the Guardian they felt "punched in the guts"; the till receipts, one suspects, will keep coming right up to the announcement. [source](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2026/nov/27/poundland-staff-administration-closures-job-losses-suppliers)

## TECH

**AI: the labs may be sitting on a cryptographic bombshell** - Scott Aaronson says he is "virtually certain" that frontier labs have made breakthroughs in the maths underpinning encryption, and that nobody has said so publicly. If true, banks and governments have months rather than the planned decade to swap the locks; if not, a great many mathematicians are having a very odd autumn. [source](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/nov/28/ai-artificial-intelligence-encryption-global-chaos)

**Linux: AMD's next graphics generation shows up in 7.5** - Initial RDNA5 enablement — the GFX13 block, new memory controller, SDMA 8 and DCN 6 display code — is heading into DRM-Next ahead of the Linux 7.5 merge window. The hardware is not out yet and the driver already knows its name, which is either good foresight or a very long pregnancy. [source](https://www.phoronix.com/news/AMD-RDNA5-Linux-7.5-Initial)
