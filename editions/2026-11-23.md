## UK NEWS

**Co-op Live emptied by an unknown substance** - Greater Manchester Police declared a major incident shortly after 10pm on Sunday when something was released into the crowd near the end of Don Toliver's set at the 12,000-capacity arena. One person was taken to hospital and around fifty were assessed by paramedics; police have not yet said what the substance was, though tear gas was the first assumption. [source](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c9e851dn8516o)

**The NHS hands the world's biggest AI screening trial its first mammograms** - Women at thirty sites, 700,000 of them over the next few years, will have one of the two radiologists who normally read each scan replaced by software. It is a £10m trial run by Warwick and Cambridge rather than a rollout, and the point is the shortage of readers as much as the promise of the model. [source](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cmpdwnw3nvzjo)

**Poundland is being prepared for mass closures** - With rescue talks fading, plans are reportedly being drawn up to shut a large slice of the 600-store estate, which employs about 12,000 people. The chain has spent a fortnight living on court extensions and its owner's released pocket money. [source](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/business/2026/11/21/poundland-braces-for-mass-closures-as-rescue-hopes-fade/)

## TECH

**AI: an AI-assisted proof claims the harder half of Navier-Stokes** - Tristan Buckmaster and Anthropic's Levent Alpöge have posted a 79-page paper, with a Lean 4 formalisation, arguing for finite-time blowup in the three-dimensional equations with no external force — the version most mathematicians regard as the actual problem. Nobody has verified it yet, and the same week produced a Riemann claim from the same quarter, so caution is the order of the day. [source](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/ai-just-solved-the-real-navier-stokes-problem/)

**AI: OpenAI's always-on Dots reach the $20 tier** - The persistent agents, which keep working between conversations and can reach into connected apps, are now being tested with a small group of ChatGPT Plus subscribers and rolled out to Business Standard workspaces. The interesting part is not the agents but the pricing: background labour at consumer rates. [source](https://www.engadget.com/2322656/openai-is-testing-dots-personal-agents-on-its-20-per-month-plus-tier/)

**Linux: 7.4-rc4 is out, and it is enormous** - Torvalds' summary for the fourth release candidate was "It's huge. What a surprise." The cycle is being driven in part by a flood of AI-generated bug reports and patches, which is either community growth or a very energetic new form of noise. [source](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Linux-7.4-rc4-Released)

**Android: Pixel Weather asks you to do the forecasting** - A weekend update adds a "report current weather" option so users can flag local conditions and, in theory, improve the forecasts for their own patch. Small, sensible, and the sort of thing that quietly makes a weather app better than a prettier one. [source](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/22/pixel-weather-report/)
