## UK news

**Burnham promises a "root-and-branch rethink of childhood" ahead of Milburn's report** — Alan Milburn publishes his review into young people and work tomorrow, and the prime minister has spent the weekend setting expectations for it, pledging early intervention years before anyone signs on and a "benefits backstop" alongside nearly half a million job and education opportunities over a decade. Almost a million young people are not in education, employment or training, so the political question is no longer whether the benefits bill gets reshaped but who absorbs the cost of it. [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/society/2026/nov/15/no-free-lunch-milburn-mcfadden-kuenssberg-uk-benefits-youth-unemployment-neet)

**NHS and fire chiefs tell ministers the zero-hours ban puts the public at risk** — Health and fire service leaders have joined forces to warn that outlawing zero-hours contracts would leave them unable to hire emergency cover without guaranteeing hours or paying for late shift changes, with NHS Employers putting the annual bill for trusts at as much as £900m. They want a rethink before the rules come in next year; the government says it is considering consultation responses. A manifesto promise meeting a rota, which never ends well for the rota. [LBC](https://www.lbc.co.uk/article/nhs-and-fire-service-leaders-warn-of-abours-zero-hours-contract-crackdown-5HjdmPC_2/)

### Derbyshire

**EMR is leasing two trains to paper over the gap left by Hitachi's late Aurora fleet** — East Midlands Railway is taking two Class 221s on lease from Grand Central from 14 December, running them as ten-carriage sets on selected weekday services between Sheffield, Nottingham and London St Pancras, worth about 17,000 extra seats a week. The cause is the delayed arrival of the Class 810 Aurora trains from Hitachi Rail, which means the county's main line to the capital gets hand-me-down rolling stock for Christmas. Derby builds trains for a living, so it will notice. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5y4jn8ywrpmo)

## Tech

**AI — OpenAI wants your agent to have a wallet, with your thumb on the trigger** — ChatGPT Wallet lets an agent complete a purchase after you approve the items, total and card via Face ID or fingerprint, with the payment network substituting a temporary card number so neither the agent nor the merchant ever sees the real one. Built with American Express, Mastercard and Visa, it starts with US Pro subscribers, and the planned next step is pre-approved spending limits. Every safeguard here is a good idea and every one will eventually be described as friction. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet/)

**AI — the Fed now counts AI as its biggest financial stability worry** — 87% of contacts in the Federal Reserve's latest survey named AI-related risk, up from 50% in May, citing complex financing arrangements, cyber threats and the possibility of a correction if AI earnings disappoint. A central bank describing your industry as its top systemic concern is the sort of citation nobody puts on the website. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/ai-rockets-top-worries-about-financial-system-latest-fed-survey-2026-11-13/)

**Linux — a busy week ends with Firefox 160, Steam's 64-bit move and RHEL 10.3** — Firefox 160 ships Mini Window, MathML in PDFs and playback-speed settings for picture-in-picture; Steam's client moved to 64-bit support; and Red Hat released RHEL 10.3 on the 6.12 kernel with GCC 16 and sealed images. Quietly, most of the desktop stack changed underneath everyone in seven days. [9to5Linux](https://9to5linux.com/9to5linux-weekly-roundup-november-15th-2026)

**Android — a week after the Pixel Drop, plenty of Pixels still haven't got it** — Google announced the November update on the 10th, and owners are still reporting no OTA, with the rollout apparently staged over several weeks. Those who do have it are on Android 17 QPR2 with roughly 33 functional fixes and a batch of new features. A staged rollout is defensible until it's your phone waiting. [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/november-pixel-drop-rollout-status-missing-features-3735295/)

**macOS — the 27.3 beta cycle rolls on** — Apple has seeded 27.3 betas across iOS, iPadOS, macOS, tvOS, visionOS and watchOS for developers, with the public release of 27.3 not expected until December. Meanwhile 27.2.1, out on the 9th, remains the version to actually run. [Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/news/)

**Hardware — the smallest Meshtastic node gets a printable case** — Seeed Studio's XIAO ESP32S3 paired with a Wio-SX1262 is about as small as a usable LoRa node gets, and there's now a 3D-printable enclosure for it that doubles as a sensor-node housing or a single-channel gateway. The sort of project that costs an evening and then quietly lives in a coat pocket for years. [Adafruit](https://blog.adafruit.com/2026/11/12/xiao-esp32s-meshtastic-kit-casing-3dthursday-3dprinting)
